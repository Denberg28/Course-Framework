# Reference catalog and validation scope

Source locations checked on **29 October 2026**, using the official agency/university/publisher pages below. These are a curated metadata catalog, not retrieved reference text. The date records this source-location review, not the effective date of a regulation.

| Source | Location | Use |
|---|---|---|
| FAA Aviation Handbooks & Manuals | https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation | Choose the handbook/chapter relevant to maintenance, flight, or other aviation topics. The index lists the 2023 General/Airframe/Powerplant handbooks and other publications. |
| CAAP Philippine Civil Aviation Regulations and Standards | https://www.caap.gov.ph/philippine-civil-aviation-regulations/ | Confirm the applicable Philippine part and current revision. |
| EASA Easy Access Rules | https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules | Confirm the applicable European CS/Part and revision; do not treat European rules as Philippine requirements. |
| MIT OpenCourseWare — Mechanics & Materials I | https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-001-mechanics-materials-i-fall-2006/ | Solid-mechanics teaching references and course notes. |
| OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1 — Stress, Strain, and Elastic Modulus | https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/12-3-stress-strain-and-elastic-modulus | University-level introductory elasticity/stress/strain reference. |

Selection is topic/context based. The app does not force aviation references into unrelated courses. Users should retain institution-approved textbooks/readings in the syllabus. Models are instructed not to invent page/section numbers, exact regulatory thresholds, or agency approval claims. Generated explanations, MCQs and answer letters are not independently validated by this catalog; instructors must check them against the actual source text and current applicability. No video suggestion feature remains in new module generation.
